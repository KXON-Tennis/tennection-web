Tennis Nut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Welcome to Tennis Nut (“the App”). This policy explains how the developer of the App (“we”) collects, uses, stores and shares your personal data. Please read it before using the App. By downloading, installing or using the App you confirm that you have read, understood and agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to run the App:

1.1 Account and profile

  • From signing in with email, Apple ID or Google: your email address, name, avatar and the provider’s identifier (Apple’s sub, Google’s user ID). If you use Apple’s Hide My Email, we receive only the relay address Apple gives us, never your real address.
  • What you enter in your profile: display name, gender, dominant hand, playing level and region.
  • If you are in Australia: your year of birth, asked once after you sign up to confirm you are 16 or older. We keep only the year, and it is never shown to others.

1.2 Activity

  • Practice logs, match records, video links and written feedback you create.
  • Your conversations with coaches and experts, Friend chat messages with your friends, and messages in hit chats and team chats.
  • Court reviews and condition reports you submit.
  • Actions such as adding friends, favouriting courts and spending nuts.
  • Content that is public by design: your bio, playing level, main racket and shoes, gear reviews, and photos you choose to add to a court page.

1.3 Location

  • With your permission, the App uses your GPS position while it is open, to show nearby courts on the Map and pre-fill the closest court when you log a practice or match.
  • Automatic check-in: while the App is open, it checks your position about every 2 minutes. If you stay within 200 m of a court for 5 minutes, it records a check-in at that court.
  • Check-ins are used to notify your friends that you have arrived at a court, and to send an anonymous notification (without your name) to players who have that court in their favourites.
  • Immersive mode: while you have it open at a court, other players can see your avatar at that court on the map.
  • Face to face: when you both open it, players within 50 m of each other can see one another to add each other as friends or record a match.
  • Challenges: some challenges use your GPS position as proof that you completed them at the court or wall.
  • You can turn location off at any time: on iPhone in Settings → Tennis Nut → Location; on Android in Settings → Apps → Tennis Nut → Permissions → Location.

1.4 Camera and photo library

  • With your permission, the App uses the camera to scan QR codes (adding friends) and to take photos for a consultation, and reads your photo library when you attach an image or video to one.
  • We never activate the camera or read the library unless you ask us to.

1.5 Push notification token

  • To deliver notifications (a coach’s reply, a message from a friend), the App obtains a device push token through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

1.6 Device and technical information

  • To improve the service and diagnose problems, the App may collect your device model, OS version, language setting, app version and crash reports.

1.7 Sharing court activity (can be turned off)

  • When this is on and you are confirmed at a court (you stayed there, or logged a practice or match there with GPS), you count towards that court’s “N playing” figure for the next 2 hours, and your display name and level appear on that court’s card and in “players around here” to players who have also played there.
  • You can turn this off at any time under Settings → Privacy → Let nearby players see me. Once off, you are not counted or shown, and you will not see other players there either.
  • Notifications about a friend arriving at the same court run on a separate path and trigger only between mutual friends.

We do not collect: contacts, text messages, health data, your Apple ID password or card details.

2. How we use your data

  • Running the service: authentication, storing your records, friend interactions, court suggestions and matching you with a coach.
  • Personalisation: using your location and records to show court activity and suggest relevant courts and content.
  • Messages and notifications: sending the notifications you have opted into, and service messages.
  • Improvement and debugging: analysing usage, fixing bugs, and preventing abuse and fraud.
  • Legal compliance: retaining or disclosing data where required by law or a judicial authority.

3. What we will not do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use your conversations, videos or location data for advertising.
  • We do not give your personal data to other users without your consent, apart from what is public in the App by design or by your choice (display name, bio, level, main racket and shoes, gear reviews, court reports and court photos).

5. Where data is stored, and for how long

  • Your data is stored on servers hosted by Supabase, in Sydney, Australia.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time under Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. Your personal data and related records are removed immediately, and third-party identifiers such as Apple’s relay email are unlinked at the same time.
  • Messages you sent in hit chats and team chats are part of a shared conversation: after you delete your account they stay in that conversation, but no longer show who you are and can't be linked back to you. The same goes for gear reviews you wrote and comments under them: they stay, signed “Unknown account”. To remove them too, delete them yourself before deleting your account, or email us. Coach accounts with consultation records need to contact us to be deleted.
  • Practice and match records are kept until you delete them.
  • Conversations, images and video in a consultation are kept until you delete your account; they remain readable in the App after the case closes.
  • Data we are legally required to keep, such as transaction records, is retained for the period the law requires.

6. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time, in the App or by writing to us:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: update or correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: delete your account yourself under Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. Some anonymised statistics, such as a court’s total visit count, may remain but can no longer be linked to you.
  • Withdrawal: revoke permissions you previously granted (location, camera, photo library, notifications).
  • Portability: request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately. Users in Australia must be 16 or older (see Terms §3); we ask for your year of birth once. If you are under 16 we don't store the year, you can't continue using the App, and you can delete your account right there.

8. International transfers

Our main database is in Sydney, Australia, some processing (gear photo background removal) runs in Taiwan (Google Cloud asia-east1), and push delivery is provided by Google Firebase, whose data centres may be located in the United States and elsewhere, so your data may be transferred across borders to those regions. We take reasonable measures to keep those transfers secure.

9. Security

  • All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS / TLS.
  • Account passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Push and session tokens are protected by the operating system’s Keychain / secure storage.
  • Even so, no networked system can be guaranteed completely secure. In the event of a significant breach we will notify affected users as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in the App or by email; continuing to use the App after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy. We suggest revisiting this page occasionally.
