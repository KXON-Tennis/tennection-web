Android · Join the Google Play test

    Android testing runs through Google Play. Tap below to fill in a short form with the Gmail(Google account) you will install with.

    Fill in the form to join ↗
  2. 2

    We add submitted addresses to the Google Play tester list regularly, usually within a day. Until then the app will not appear in Play for you.

  3. 3

    Once you are on the list, open our test link withthe Google account you submitted, tap “Become a tester”, then install from Play.

  4. 4

    Open Tennis Nut and sign in

    New versions arrive through Google Playautomatically — no more manual downloads.

🔒 Official Google Play testing: installs and updates come from the store, so no “unknown sources” permission and no Play Protect warnings.
