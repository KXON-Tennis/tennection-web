v1.31 · Immersive mode, gear photos, and hours on every racket

This post covers both 1.29 and 1.31. See nearby players in immersive mode, turn racket and shoe photos into catalog shots, and keep the racket, strings and tension with every practice and match.

🕶️

Immersive mode

A 3D map from the center button, showing nearby players who have it open. Tap someone to add them, record your match or plan the next one.

📸

Gear photos

Tap a card in your bag and choose Take a photo. About a minute later it becomes a clean catalog-style photo that friends see on your player card.

🎾

Hours on every racket

Practices and matches use your main racket and shoes, and keep the strings and tension you played with. Compare hours and wins for each string setup.

🗂️

Records and consultations moved

The tab bar now has Map and Records. Practices and matches share one timeline. Consultations moved to My consultations in your passport.

📊

Records counted by set

Casual match records and win rates now count sets; tournaments still count matches.

Also fixed

  • Everything updates the moment you save
  • Room for 10 rackets and 6 pairs of shoes
  • Fixed an occasional Android map crash
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
