v1.12.4 · You can cancel a hit now

The host's cancel control was an unlabelled grey icon, and people didn't read it as cancel. It is now a button that says so — and the row on your home screen can cancel too.

🗓

You can cancel a hit now

Hosts saw a small icon with no label, while people who had joined saw “I'm going” and “Can't make it”. All three are you taking back your own decision, but they looked nothing alike. Hosts now get a button with words on it. The “Upcoming hits” row on the home screen can cancel as well, so you don't have to open the court and find it again.

⏱

Taking back your free time asks once

That button sat right beside Edit, and a mis-tap deleted the slot outright. It now shows you which hours you are about to remove, and asks again.

🎥

Coaches can send ten-minute videos

Three minutes is not enough to walk someone through a change to their stroke, so the per-video limit on the coach side is now 10 minutes. For players it stays at 2 videos a month, 30 seconds each. To send something longer, paste a YouTube or Google Drive link — coaches can open those too.

🏅

Every team has its own colour

Teams without a crest used to look identical on the map: the same blue circle with one character inside. Each team now has its own fill, and the blue ring stays — the ring says “this is a team”, the fill says which one.

Also fixed

  • Layouts no longer spill past the edge on some screens when the keyboard opens
  • Learning saves and Pre-match review have a back button when opened from a notification
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
