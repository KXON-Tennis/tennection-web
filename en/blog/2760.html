v1.35 · Challenge levels, Ask players, and free time that finds a match

Every challenge stage now shows its level, and when you finish with a partner they witness it and you both pass together. Add a question to a practice for friends and nearby players to answer. Weekly free time repeats on its own, and the app finds players whose time and level fit.

🏆

Challenge levels and new stages

Every stage is tagged Beginner to Advanced, so you can start wherever fits. New wall volley and line-change rally challenges, plus 10-ball starter stages for rally and volley. When you finish with a partner, they witness it and you both pass together; passing with different partners shows as ×N. Passing earns nuts, and jumping to a higher stage counts the ones below it too.

🙋

Ask players

Add a one-line question to a practice, with an optional video. Friends, teammates and players in your area see it on Home and can reply. The practice keeps an “Ask · N replies” line so you can always find the thread.

📅

Free time that finds a match

Weekly free time repeats on its own; just pick the days. The app matches you by level, singles or doubles, and time, and Home shows how many players overlap with you. On the map, courts that fit your free time glow green.

🎾

Better court cards

A section bar takes you straight to who's playing, hits, free time and court info. When you're at the court, one tap reports its condition.

🪪

A smoother first day

Onboarding is one path, and you choose up front whether others can invite you to play. When pinning your usual court, pick from nearby or popular.

Also fixed

  • Hit times default to now, in 10-minute steps
  • Edit year and model when duplicating a racket or shoes
  • Gear forms start with a photo, cut out automatically
  • Saving shows the nuts you actually earned
  • The map's Today lens shows how many players are elsewhere
  • Clearer steps for sharing challenge videos from YouTube or Drive
  • Other players' passports show only the stages they've passed
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
