v1.46–1.47 · Serve Kaleidoscope and stealth mode

A new serve challenge: before every serve, draw a random side, target and spin, and it only counts if it goes in as drawn. Stealth mode for quiet practice, and Home shows who's playing now.

🎰

Serve Kaleidoscope

Before every serve, a slot-style draw picks your side (deuce/ad), target (wide/body/T) and spin (flat/slice/kick). It only counts if it goes in as drawn. Miss, and you start over. Stages at 1, 3, 5, 7 and 10 in a row. The demo animation follows each draw, and every serve of your best run is saved, so your witness can see it too.

🕶

Stealth mode

Only friends, teammates, hits you join and people you've met face to face can see you. Your practices, challenges and footprints still count. Your name just doesn't show to strangers.

🟢

See who's playing now

Friends' activity on Home now counts from when a session ended, and shows Playing now while it's still going.

🎾

Two-handed forehand, full bios

Tick Two-handed forehand on your passport. Bios expand to show in full. Gear photos that don't cut out well are marked for a retake, and unsent photos resend automatically.

🛰

Government aerial imagery

In Taiwan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Japan and NSW, zooming in overlays local government aerial photos, with the source named in the map menu. The footprint globe now has a night view.

Also fixed

  • Android tablets are supported
  • Links in consultation messages are tappable
  • Fixes for sharing on some iPhones and crashes on some system languages
  • Profile details that failed to save from Nov 18 to 20 save normally again, with no update needed
