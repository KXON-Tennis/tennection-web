v1.23.0 · Talk it over in the hit chat

Everyone in a hit now has a chat of their own, and the whole group hears when someone drops out. Völkl and ProKennex rackets are in too.

💬

Hit chat

Once you've joined a hit, there's a chat button on your upcoming hits, on the court's hit list and on the day-of card. Who brings balls, how to split the court fee, running late, where to meet: say it there. It opens once someone joins, only people in the hit can see it, and it closes 3 hours after the hit ends.

📣

Everyone hears when someone drops out

Tapping "Not going" used to notify only the host. Now everyone in the hit gets a notification, and a line appears in the chat.

🙋

A simpler day-of card

The card that appears 2 hours before a hit now has just two buttons: "I'm here" and the chat. The list says it plainly: "KC is here".

🗺️

See how busy a court has been

Courts with someone playing now also show how many came today, like "1 playing · 5 today".

🎾

More racket brands

Völkl and ProKennex are now on the list. Brand and year are no longer pre-filled; leave the year blank if you're not sure.

Also fixed

  • On iPhone, tap outside a text field to close the keyboard
  • Switching accounts on the same phone now updates your hits correctly
  • Availability lists now show the soonest slots first
  • Check-in range is back to 200 m, so every court at a large venue counts (since 1.22.0)
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
