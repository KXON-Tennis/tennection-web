v1.44–1.45 · A practice timer, and pick your own home court

Start a practice and the timer runs, tracking time on each racket. Pick your own home court and swipe through court photos. New 2C1D and RDC volley challenges, and you can message new friends right away.

⏱

Practice timer

Tap Start practice at the court and the timer runs — pause it or switch rackets along the way. When you finish it becomes a practice record, no guessing how long you played. Each racket adds up its real playing time, so your bag shows which one you use most.

🏠

Pick your home court

Tap Home court on your player card and choose from courts you've played. Leave it on auto to keep using your most-played court.

📷

Swipe through court photos

Court photos swipe, with a counter and arrows. The photo viewer has a close button, and tapping the photo closes it too. Photos load at display size across the app, so scrolling and swiping are smoother.

🎯

New challenges: 2C1D and RDC volleys

Two new challenges, and the rally challenge adds 200 and 250-ball stages. Challenges are grouped into groundstrokes, volleys, serves and practice walls, each with a progress bar. Demo animations were redrawn and label Cross, Down the line and Sideline.

💬

Message new friends right away

Once you're friends you can start talking, even before they open chat.

Also fixed

  • Serve challenges renamed to one-side and both-sides; rally naming unified
  • Court status reporting opens straight to the options at a court
  • Court cards list Played here and Other courts around here separately, with matching counts
  • New players see recent activity when pinning a favourite court
  • No more stray Chinese in the English interface
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store or Google Play.
