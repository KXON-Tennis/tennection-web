v1.28.0 · Share cards, redesigned around one number

Minutes played, the match score, or which court you just conquered: each card now leads with one number. New: My court map, every court you've played on one shareable map.

🖼️

Practice: how long you played

Share cards are redesigned. A practice card leads with the minutes you played, then this week's session count, the court, and your player number there. The last line reads "With my" and your main racket. Photos now fill the card automatically, and you can still pinch and drag. Post 3:4, Story 9:16 and transparent sticker are all there.

🏆

Match: the score

Match cards put each set's score side by side, with win or loss and singles or doubles on top, then this week's count, your opponents and the court. Doubles also names your partner.

🚩

New court: which number

The new-court card leads with which court this is for you. Your first check-in at a court now shows the "Court #N" celebration too, so you can make the card right there. Before, only logging a practice did.

🗺️

My court map

In your passport, open Conquered courts and tap Map to see every court you've played, with counts of courts, countries, cities and surfaces. Tap Share my court map to turn it into a card.

📤

Share later, still easy to find

Make a share card, under your passport ID card, lists your latest practice, match, newest court and court map. With no records yet, it takes you to courts nearby.

👥

Share today's team

When two or more teammates log at the same court on the same day, the team page offers a card or sticker with the team name, how many came and where, with your team logo on top.

🎾

Also on this racket

Rackets and shoes in your bag show how many other players use them. Open the list to see friends and players from your city, and read their reviews.

Also fixed

  • Update reminders when a new version is out; on Android it downloads in the app
  • "My courts" in the diary is now "Checked-in courts", before Saved
  • One Share button in the same place on practices, matches and courts
  • Forms say which field is missing and why they can't be sent
  • Gear counts now read "2 using now, 2 used before"
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
