v1.48 · Visitor info for playing abroad

The map has a new Visitor info view: pick a city and see the courts visitors can actually play, with prices and how to book. Players who went can leave a tip for the next one.

✈️

Where do you want to play?

Tap Visitor info on the map and pick a destination: Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, Taipei, Fukuoka, Okinawa, Kyoto, Sapporo, Nagoya or Hong Kong. The map flies there and the list shows only courts visitors can actually get on. Each court says how: book with the venue, free walk-in rotation, membership first, register at the counter first, or play with a local group. Visitor price, how to book and the official site are on the court card. Names come in two lines, one you can read and one in the local language to show a local.

💬

Visitor tips

Players who went can leave a one-line tip for the next visitor, no check-in needed; those who checked in are marked. Anyone can upvote or downvote, and heavily downvoted tips fold away.

🏠

Locals can add visitor info

When you edit a court in your own country, there's a new question: how does a visitor get in? Once reviewed, the court card says it came from a local player.

🏙

3D on the map

Tap 3D on the flat map to tilt in place and see buildings and landmarks, like Tokyo Tower next to Shiba Park. With a court card open, it flies to that court.

📣

Opening a hit to everyone notifies the new audience

Change a hit from friends or team to public and the people who can now see it get a notification. No need to post it again.

Also fixed

  • Visitor prices show in local currency (HK$, A$, S$)
  • With the visitor list open, you can still move the map, switch basemaps and locate yourself
  • Tapping a row flies the map there and circles the court
  • New courts on the map in Fukuoka, Busan, Kyoto, Sapporo and Aichi
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store or Google Play.
