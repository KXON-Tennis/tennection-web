v1.26.0 · Edit your hits, and teams get a chat

Hosts can now change a hit after posting it, and open it up to more people. Teams have their own chat, and joining now goes through the captain. You can also ask a player to write about their racket or shoes.

✏️

Edit a hit after posting

Hosts can change the time, what you'll play, the ball, the level and the note. You can also invite more people or let more people see it. Widening who can see it asks you to confirm first: anyone who has already seen it or joined keeps seeing it. Hit cards on the home screen and on courts now put the details on top and the buttons underneath.

💬

Team chat

Every team has its own chat, visible only to members. Practice times, who's bringing balls, where to play this week: say it there.

🤝

Joining a team is now a request

Add a line when you ask to join; a suggested line is one tap away. The captain approves it right on the team page, and you'll get a notification. Anyone with the invite code can still join directly, and the captain is notified.

🎾

See what racket and shoes people use

The list of players at a court now shows their main racket and shoes. Curious about one? Raise your hand. The owner only sees how many people asked, not who, and you'll be notified when they write about it. When adding a racket, you now pick the model from a list instead of scanning a photo.

🏠

Home court on your player card

Your passport's player card now shows the court you've played at most in the last 90 days. Nothing to fill in: your home court is where you actually play.

👋

Say hi when adding someone new

Friend requests to players you don't know yet now come with a short line, with a suggestion one tap away. They see why you want to connect, not just a name.

Also fixed

  • Fixed a crash the first time the app opened after a fresh install on iPhone
  • Links in hit chats can be tapped
  • In Today's footprints, courts are no longer merged into one circle
  • Levels now come first in player lists, so long court names don't cut them off
  • No more Chinese text in the English interface, and wording is consistent
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
