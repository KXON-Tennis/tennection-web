v1.15.0 · Ask the people free around you

Your home screen lists free-time windows from players in the cities you play in — level and bio included. You can now ask them directly.

👋

Players free nearby — now askable

Free-time windows posted by people in the cities you play in, with their level and a line of their bio. Deciding whether to ask someone you have never met comes down to those one or two lines. The list used to be read-only. Now they get “you asked them”, not an announcement.

📋

“See the week” is a list, showing everyone

It opens as a list now, not a timetable — what you want is who and when. The calendar is one tap away, top right. It also shows everyone by default instead of friends only.

🗺

The all-players conquest map

Passport’s Court Conquest now opens a map of every court Nut players have reached, with your own share counted. Court pages show how many players have played there.

📅

Dates read 9/24 (Wed)

“Free on Thursday” never said which Thursday.

Also fixed

  • Hybrid-strung rackets: both mains and crosses can be picked in gear posts
  • Developer chat updates on its own
  • “N more free nearby” on the home screen is tappable
  • On iOS, a refused photo-library save falls back to the share sheet
  • Fixed a few court coordinates so on-site check-in stops failing
How do I update? Android updates itself through Google Play; on iPhone, update from the App Store. Not installed yet? Get it on the App Store.
